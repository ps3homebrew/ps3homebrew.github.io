# Getting the best PS3

Selecting the best PS3 depends on the things you valuate the most:

* Power efficiency
* [PS2 compatibility](compatibility/ps2)
* Reliability
* [CFW compatibility](compatibility/cfw)
* Price and availability
* Features
* Look and feel

Each PS3 model has its own advantages and disadvantages.

* For the best default [PS2 compatibility](compatibility/ps2), but worst price, reliability, price and availability, get a [CECHAxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHAxx).
It has the PS2's EE (Emotion Engine), GS (Graphics Synthesizer) and Rambus on board, making [PS2 compatibility](compatibility/ps2) almost perfect, but at a stock resolution. 

* [CECHBxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHBxx) consoles are similar; they have a smaller hard drive and a full black look, but no WiFi capabilities. 
This model is even rarer since it was quickly discontinued, due to that it can be even pricer than a [CECHAxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHAxx), so for most people it isn't worth it.

* [CECHCxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHCxx) and [CECHExx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHExx) only have the PS2's GS (Graphics Synthesizer) on the board, and due to that it has worse [PS2 compatibility](compatibility/ps2) (it's partially emulated), but it can upscale PS2 games up to 1080p, and modders have been fixing most of the [PS2 compatibility](compatibility/ps2) issues.
They're a bit more reliable than [CECHAxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHAxx) and [CECHBxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHBxx) and usually have better prices and availability, this is probably what you wanna get as the best PS3 in general if you can buy it.

The models mentioned above suffer from having NEC/Tokins and a 90nm RSX which are prone to failure causing the famous [YLOD](problems\ylod).
Getting a [NEC/Tokins replacement](mods\nec-tokins) along with the [Frankenstein mod](mods\frankenstein) plus a [delid](mods\delid) is the best idea.
[Undervolting](mods\undervolting) is a good thing to do to these systems too to reduce heat and consumption, but they're still pretty power consuming.
Keep in mind doing these mods to existing consoles can be expensive depending on your location. Already modded ones are usually a better and cheaper option.

* If you only care about having a Fat PS3 cause of its looking, then you can get any Fat going from [CECHGxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHGxx) to [CECHQxx](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECHQxx). 
They have a bit more reliable 65nm RSX, but they can still fail due to that GPU and they still have NEC/Tokins inside.

* If you're looking for a cheaper reliable option, not PS2 compatible, but still CFW compatible, you'll likely want to get a [CECH-25XX](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECH-25xx) with a Datecode below 1B, or a [CECH-21XX](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECH-251xx) if you can't look up the Datecode or the previous model isn't available for you.
This models are the last ones on supporting full CFW. They have a 40nm RSX and no NEC/Tokins, so they're very reliable. 
Keep in mind [CECH-25XX](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECH-25xx) might be harder to [delid](mods\delid), but delidding shouldn't be necessary.

* If you want to just get the cheapest, most available, and usually most reliable option, but not PS2 nor CFW compatible buy a Super Slim (anything between [CECH-40XX](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECH-40xx) and [CECH-43XX](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECH-43xx)).
They have the best cooling system, most of them have a 28nm RSX except for most [CECH-40XX](https://www.psdevwiki.com/ps3/CECH-40xx) consoles.
Between those Super Slim models it is suggested to not get an eMMC one (labeled as 12GB) if possible, as it's a small and non-reliable memory. [qCFW](installing-qcfw) compatibility isn't as good too.
However, if it's in a good price and available for you, don't doubt too much. They're still good consoles, and its storage can be expanded.
The defect these models have is the BT/WiFi module, where it is possible that it simply dies, locking you out of updates and those features.

Without [PS2 hardware compatibility](compatibility/ps2), you can still play PS2 games via emulation, but you'll lack the physical disc support, and the compatibility will be worse.
