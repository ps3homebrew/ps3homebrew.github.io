## What is Homebrew?

[**Homebrew**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_homebrew_video_games) usually refers to software that is not authorized by Sony. It allows you to run homebrew games, tools like save editing and backup, and emulators for various older systems.

## What is Custom Firmware?

**Custom Firmware** ("CFW") is a modified version of the Sony's PS3 official firmware that allows the execution of unsigned code without the need to use a homebrew enabler, along with additional tools.

## What does this guide install?

This guide has the end goal of taking advantage of everything a PS3 can do.

## What can I do with Custom Firmware?

* Customize your home screen with user-created themes.
* Use "ROM hacks" for games that you own and load custom plugins.
* Resign save files.
* Play games for older systems with various emulators, using RetroArch or other standalone emulators.
* Dump your game discs to a format you can install, and play them without needing the disc.
* Stream games from your PC to your console.
* Play PS1, PS2 and PSP games with Sony's emulators, with additional patches and configs for better performance and compatibility.

## What do I need to know before starting?

* **Before beginning the guide, you must know the risks of PS3 hacking: EVERY time you modify your system, there is always the potential for an UNRECOVERABLE brick. They're rare, but still a possibility so make sure you follow ALL directions EXACTLY.**
* All PS3 consoles can be jailbroken, but what you need to do and the potential of it depends on your model.
* If everything goes according to plan, you will lose no data and end up with everything that you started with (games, PSN Account, saves, etc. will be preserved).
* **Keep your device plugged in throughout the entire process to avoid data loss or damage from an unexpected power-off!**
